### 3 Must-Visit Places in North Goa

1.  **Vagator Beach & Chapora Fort:**
    *   **Why:** Vagator offers stunning cliffside views, two distinct sections (Ozran/Little Vagator and Big Vagator), and is known for its beautiful sunsets. Perched atop a hill overlooking Vagator, Chapora Fort offers panoramic vistas of the Arabian Sea and the surrounding beaches, famously featured in the movie "Dil Chahta Hai." It's perfect for photos and soaking in the Goan landscape.
    *   **Vibe:** Scenic, relaxed during the day, lively in the evening with shacks.
2.  **Anjuna Beach:**
    *   **Why:** A classic Goan beach, Anjuna is known for its lively atmosphere, beach shacks, and its famous Flea Market (if your visit falls on a Wednesday). It's great for swimming, sunbathing, and experiencing the bohemian side of Goa.
    *   **Vibe:** Bustling, bohemian, good for people-watching.
3.  **Morjim Beach:**
    *   **Why:** Often called "Little Russia" due to its large Russian expatriate population, Morjim is significantly quieter and more serene than Anjuna or Vagator. It's known for its clean sands, gentle waves, and being a nesting site for Olive Ridley turtles (though seeing them is rare). It's perfect for a relaxing stroll, a peaceful swim, and enjoying a beautiful, unobstructed sunset.
    *   **Vibe:** Tranquil, calm, clean, good for relaxation.

## 2-Day Weekend Itinerary

**Friday Evening (Arrival):**
*   **6:00 PM - 7:00 PM:** Arrive in Goa (MOPA Airport or Madgaon/Vasco Railway Station).
*   **7:00 PM - 8:30 PM:** Take a taxi/pre-booked cab to your hotel in Anjuna/Vagator.
    *   *Budget Tip:* Pre-book an airport taxi or try the Goa Miles app for potentially better rates than local prepaid counters right outside the airport. If possible, consider getting a shared taxi if others are going your way.
*   **8:30 PM - 9:30 PM:** Check into your hotel, freshen up.
*   **9:30 PM onwards:** Head out for a relaxed dinner at a local eatery or beach shack near your hotel. Explore the immediate vicinity.
    *   *Dinner Suggestion:* Curlies or Shiva Valley (Vagator/Anjuna) for ambiance, or a smaller local place for cheaper, authentic food.

**Day 1: Saturday - Forts, Beaches & Sunsets**
*   **9:00 AM - 10:00 AM:** Breakfast at a local cafe or your hotel.
*   **10:00 AM - 10:30 AM:** Rent a scooter (approx. Rs 400-500/day, bargain slightly). This is crucial for budget and flexibility.
*   **10:30 AM - 12:30 PM:** Ride to **Chapora Fort**. Explore the ruins, take photos, and soak in the panoramic views.
*   **12:30 PM - 2:00 PM:** Head down to **Vagator Beach**. Relax, swim, enjoy the atmosphere.
*   **2:00 PM - 3:00 PM:** Lunch at a beach shack on Vagator Beach (Goan fish thali, prawn curry, or basic fried rice/noodles).
*   **3:00 PM - 6:00 PM:** Drive to **Anjuna Beach**. Walk along the shore, browse any small stalls (if not Flea Market day), take a swim.
*   **6:00 PM - 7:00 PM:** Find a good spot at either Anjuna or Vagator Beach to watch the beautiful Goan sunset.
*   **7:30 PM onwards:** Dinner at a popular cafe in Anjuna/Vagator (e.g., German Bakery, Baba Au Rhum for a slightly higher but still reasonable budget) or another beach shack. Enjoy a couple of drinks.
    *   *Budget Tip:* Buy your own beers/spirits from a local liquor store before going to the beach for significant savings on drinks.

**Day 2: Sunday - Serenity & Departure**
*   **9:00 AM - 10:00 AM:** Breakfast.
*   **10:00 AM - 12:00 PM:** Drive north towards **Morjim Beach**. Enjoy the scenic ride through local villages.
*   **12:00 PM - 2:00 PM:** Relax at Morjim Beach. It's much quieter, perfect for a peaceful stroll, reading, or a calm swim.
*   **2:00 PM - 3:00 PM:** Lunch at one of the charming beach shacks in Morjim (they often have excellent seafood).
*   **3:00 PM - 4:30 PM:** If time and energy permit, you could briefly visit **Ashwem Beach** (just north of Morjim) for a similar serene vibe, or simply enjoy more time at Morjim.
*   **4:30 PM - 6:00 PM:** Drive back to your hotel. Freshen up, pack your bags.
*   **6:00 PM - 6:30 PM:** Return your scooter.
*   **6:30 PM - 7:30 PM:** Early dinner near your hotel or grab a quick bite on your way to the airport/station.
*   **7:30 PM onwards:** Head to the airport/station for your departure.

### Budget Saving Tips:

*   **Eat Local:** Opt for local Goan thalis, street food, and fresh seafood from beach shacks rather than upscale restaurants.
*   **Scooter is King:** Renting a scooter is the most cost-effective way to get around North Goa. Always wear helmets!
*   **Drink Smart:** Buy water bottles and any alcoholic beverages from local shops (liquor stores) rather than at shacks or restaurants where markups are higher.
*   **Bargain:** For scooter rentals, souvenirs, and sometimes even taxi fares, a bit of polite bargaining can save you money.
*   **Avoid Peak Season/Holidays:** Prices for everything (accommodation, food, travel) skyrocket during peak season (mid-December to January) and public holidays. Visiting in shoulder seasons (October-November, February-March) offers better weather and prices.

Have an amazing and memorable trip to Goa with your friend!
